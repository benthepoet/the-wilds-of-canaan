# The Wilds of Canaan
## Combat, Weapons & Stats Document

---

## 1. Philosophy

Combat in The Wilds of Canaan is not heroic. It is desperate, positional, and often avoidable. You are not a warrior cleansing the land. You are a survivor holding a candle in a dark forest. Most hostiles will kill you in a fair fight.

**Core principles:**
- **Asymmetric by Vocation.** A Woodsman, Priest, and Craftsman do not fight the same way.
- **Gear over levels.** There are no experience points or character levels. Progression is finding better tools and learning when to use them.
- **Positioning is everything.** Luring a Wendigo into a Circle of Salt, kiting a wolf pack through a trap line, or channeling an exorcism from behind a palisade wall.
- **Degradation.** All weapons and armor degrade with use. The Craftsman is not optional for sustained combat.
- **Fear is a mechanic.** Some enemies do not just deal damage. They corrupt, they freeze you in place, they make you drop your weapon.

---

## 2. Core Stats

There are **six primary stats.** No Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution. Your body is assumed capable; your will and equipment determine survival.

| Stat | Range | Function |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Health** | 0-100 | Physical damage. At 0, you die. |
| **Faith** | 0-100 | Mental/spiritual resilience. Priest's "mana." Affects holy resistance and exorcism potency. At 0, you panic (cannot act for 2 turns). |
| **Stamina** | 0-100 | Exhaustion. Sprinting, heavy attacks, and blocking drain this. At 0, you cannot sprint or power attack. Recovers slowly or by resting. |
| **Corruption** | 0-100 | Spiritual taint. Gained from blighted wounds, cursed enemies, or dark areas. At 25+, you take +20% damage from hostiles. At 50+, hostiles can sense you through walls. At 100, you become a Revenant (game over). |
| **Warmth** | 0-100 | Environmental. Affects stamina regen and max Health. Not a combat stat directly, but winter combat is brutal without it. |
| **Hunger** | 0-100 | Starvation reduces max Stamina and slows Health regen. |

**No level system.** Your starting stats are identical across all Vocations. The difference is how you fight, not how strong you are.

## 3. The Three Combat Styles

### 3.1 The Woodsman: The Hunter

The Woodsman is the **direct combatant,** but he is still fragile. He wins through preparation, speed, and first strikes. He loses fair fights.

**Combat advantages:**
- **Read the Woods:** Reveals enemy patrol paths and ambush points before engagement.
- **First Strike:** Attacking an enemy that has not detected you deals +50% damage and bypasses their first action.
- **Sprint:** Moves 2 tiles per turn (costs 20 Stamina). Can disengage from most hostiles.
- **Trap Mastery:** Traps placed by the Woodsman deal +30% damage and are hidden from hostiles until triggered.
- **Bow Expertise:** No accuracy penalty at range. Arrows retrieved from corpses 50% of the time.

**Combat disadvantages:**
- Cannot consecrate ground. Fighting in blight slowly raises Corruption.
- No healing abilities. Wounds must be treated with salves or rest.
- Light armor only. Heavy armor slows him too much.
- Low Faith. Susceptible to fear effects and demonic presence.

**The Woodsman does not tank.** He stalks, strikes, and vanishes. His ideal fight is one arrow from the edge of a clearing, then retreat into forest he knows better than his prey.

---

### 3.2 The Priest: The Ward-Keeper

The Priest does not swing a sword. He **denies ground, banishes evil, and outlasts.** His "combat" is area control and attrition.

**Combat advantages:**
- **Holy Ground:** Hostiles take damage per turn while standing on consecrated tiles. Demons and undead take double damage.
- **Banishment:** AoE pushback. Stuns demons for 1-3 turns. Costs Faith, not Stamina.
- **Bless Weapon:** Temporarily converts any weapon to silver-equivalent vs. demons. Lasts 3 turns.
- **Circle of Salt:** Hostiles cannot cross. Creates chokepoints. Dissolves in rain.
- **Last Rites:** Instantly destroys Revenants and Drowned. No other class can do this.
- **High Faith:** Immune to panic from most fear effects.

**Combat disadvantages:**
- **Fragile.** Light robes. Health pool is standard but he has no armor skills.
- **Channeling vulnerability.** Banishment and Grand Rites require 2-3 turns of uninterrupted channeling. If hit, the rite fails and Faith is lost.
- **No ranged attacks** (without cross-class learning).
- **Dependent on terrain.** Without Holy Ground, he is just a man in a robe holding a stick.

**The Priest turns the battlefield into a sanctuary.** He does not chase enemies. He makes them come to him, through pain, and die on consecrated earth.

---

### 3.3 The Craftsman: The Siege

The Craftsman does not fight with grace. He fights with **architecture, chemistry, and preparation.** His combat happens before the enemy arrives.

**Combat advantages:**
- **Traps:** Pit traps, silver stake traps, tripwires, and clockwork snares. All deal high damage and cost the enemy their turn.
- **Fortify:** Makes a structure immune to damage for one night. The enemy must break the door or starve him out.
- **Siege Weapons:** Mounted scissor traps, counterweighted spikes, and ballistae (late-game). Built into architecture.
- **Armor Expertise:** Wears heavy armor with reduced Stamina penalty. Can block attacks with a shield.
- **Repair:** Fixes weapons and armor mid-fight (if he brought a hammer).

**Combat disadvantages:**
- **Slow.** Heavy armor + low base speed = 1 tile per turn. Cannot outrun anything.
- **No holy damage.** Cannot hurt demons without silver weapons or traps.
- **Dependent on ground.** Traps must be placed in advance. A surprise attack leaves him defenseless.
- **No healing.** Must rely on salves and food like the Woodsman.

**The Craftsman builds the killing field.** His ideal fight is a wolf pack charging his palisade, falling into pits, impaling themselves on spikes, and dying on his doorstep while he repairs the wall behind them.

## 5. The Damage Types

There are four damage types. Enemies have vulnerabilities and resistances.

| Type | Source | Effective Against | Resisted By |
|------|--------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Physical** | Clubs, spears, axes, traps, claws | Beasts, weak undead | Armored demons, stone constructs |
| **Silver** | Silver mace, blessed weapons, silver traps | Demons, undead, Wendigo | Beasts (no bonus) |
| **Holy** | Banishment, consecrated ground, blessed water | Demons, shadows, Drowned | Beasts (immune) |
| **Corruption** | Blighted weapons, cursed ground, some enemy attacks | Living players (raises Corruption stat) | Undead (heals them) |

**Example:** A Corrupted Wolf (`w`) is a beast with blight taint. Physical damage works normally. Silver does nothing extra. Holy does nothing. But if it wounds you, the wound is **Corruption-type**, raising your Corruption stat.

**Example:** A Wendigo (`W`) is a demon-beast hybrid. Physical works but it has high health. Silver deals +50%. Holy deals +100% but only on consecrated ground. It deals Corruption damage with every attack.

## 6. Enemy Combat Behaviors

Each enemy type has a distinct combat AI that forces different tactics.

### 6.1 Beast-Type (Wolves, Bears, Corrupted Deer)

- **Pack tactics:** Wolves surround before attacking. They coordinate.
- **Charge:** Bears and stags charge in a straight line. If they miss, they are stunned for 1 turn.
- **Berserk:** Below 30% health, beasts attack twice per turn but lose coordination.
- **Fear fire:** Beasts will not enter a tile adjacent to a brazier or firepit unless desperate.

**Counter:** Woodsman bow kiting, Craftsman pit traps, Priest holy braziers.

### 6.2 Demon-Type (Wendigo, Shadows, The Hollow Stag)

- **Mimicry:** Wendigo mimics human screams to lure you off holy ground.
- **Phase:** Shadows can move through walls and obstacles. Only visible in light.
- **Corruption aura:** Standing within 3 tiles of a demon raises Corruption by 1 per turn.
- **Fear effect:** Demons cause panic (cannot act) on a failed Faith check.
- **Holy vulnerability:** Demons take damage per turn on consecrated ground. They will path around it if possible.

**Counter:** Priest banishment, silver weapons, Circle of Salt, staying on holy ground.

### 6.3 Undead-Type (Drowned, Revenants)

- **Relentless:** Undead do not tire, do not panic, and do not retreat.
- **Grab:** Drowned attempt to drag the player into water. If successful, the player drowns in 3 turns unless they break free (Stamina check).
- **Rise again:** Revenants (dead players/NPCs) rise 1 day after death unless Last Rites are performed.
- **Water bond:** Drowned regenerate health while in water.

**Counter:** Priest Last Rites (instant kill), silver mace, staying out of water, burning corpses.

### 6.4 Manifestations (Bosses)

Manifestations are not fought with standard combat. They are **environmental puzzles** where damage is only one component.

**The Drowned Choir:**
- Spawns Drowned continuously.
- Must be lured onto dry land to take damage.
- The Priest must perform a 3-minute Banishment rite while the Choir is on consecrated ground.
- The Woodsman can snipe from the shore with silver arrows.
- The Craftsman can build a reinforced dock that limits where the Choir can spawn.

**The Hollow Stag:**
- Charges in a line, corrupting all ground it crosses.
- Summons blight fog every 3 turns.
- The Priest can trap it in a Circle of Salt.
- The Woodsman can kite it through a forest of pre-placed traps.
- The Craftsman can build a palisade gate that forces the Stag to charge into a spike wall.

**The Burning Eye (Final Boss):**
- Does not move. It is the center of the arena.
- Fires a beam attack every 4 turns that deals 50 Holy damage (only avoidable by cover).
- Spawns all enemy types simultaneously.
- The entire arena is blight (100). Holy Ground decays twice as fast.
- **This is not a damage race.** It is a survival endurance test. The victory condition is completing the final rite/escape/seal, not killing the Eye.

## 7. Status Effects & Conditions

| Condition | Cause | Effect | Cure |
|-----------|-------|--------|------|
| **Bleeding** | Physical wounds, bear claws | -2 Health per turn. Stamina regen halved. | Bandage, rest, time |
| **Blighted Wound** | Corrupted enemy attack, blight tile | -1 Health per turn. +1 Corruption per turn. | Holy Water, Priest healing, Confessional |
| **Panicked** | Demon fear effect, Faith 0, Wendigo scream | Cannot act for 2 turns. Drop held weapon. | Faith recovery, holy ground, time |
| **Frozen** | Winter combat, lake ice, Hollow Stag blizzard | Movement reduced to 1 tile. Attack speed halved. | Fire, warmth, shelter |
| **Grabbed** | Drowned, some traps | Cannot move. Take damage each turn. Must break free (Stamina check). | Break free, ally attack, silver stake |
| **Cursed** | Rare enemy attack, cursed item | All stats reduced by 20%. Hostiles detect you from double range. | Priest exorcism, rare herb, cathedral blessing |
| **Exhausted** | Stamina 0, overexertion | Cannot sprint. Attack damage -30%. Stamina regen stopped. | Rest, food, warmth |
| **Starving** | Hunger 0 | Max Stamina reduced by 50%. Health does not regenerate. | Food |
| **Corrupted** (not the stat) | Blight tile 80+, cursed enemy kill | Vision reduced. Hallucinations (false enemy symbols appear). | Purifying fire, Priest confession, leaving blight |

## 8. Combat Turn Structure

Combat is **turn-based** on the grid. The player always acts first in a new encounter (if they detected the enemy). If the enemy ambushes the player, the enemy acts first.

### 8.1 Player Turn

**Action points:** You have **2 action points** per turn.

| Action | Cost | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| Move | 1 | 1 tile (2 if sprinting, costs 20 Stamina) |
| Attack (light) | 1 | Normal damage, normal speed |
| Attack (heavy) | 2 | +50% damage, -10 Stamina |
| Use item | 1 | Holy Water, bandage, bait, etc. |
| Channel rite | 2 | Priest only. Begin or continue a rite. |
| Place trap | 2 | Craftsman only. Must be adjacent. |
| Repair | 2 | Craftsman only. Fix weapon or armor. |
| Block | 1 (reaction) | Declare before enemy acts. Negates one melee attack. Costs 15 Stamina. |
| Flee | 2 | Attempt to break engagement. Success based on Stamina vs. enemy speed. |

**Free actions (no cost):**
- Swap weapon
- Check inventory
- Look/examine
- Toggle sprint mode (affects next move)

### 8.2 Enemy Turn

Enemies act based on their AI type:
- **Beasts:** Move + attack, or double move if closing distance.
- **Demons:** Move + special (scream, phase, corruption aura), or move + attack.
- **Undead:** Always move toward player. Always attack if adjacent. Never flee.
- **Bosses:** Unique patterns per Manifestation.

### 8.3 Elevation & Terrain Modifiers

| Terrain | Movement | Combat Effect |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| **Forest** | Normal (Woodsman: fast) | Blocks line of sight. Ranged attacks -2 range. |
| **Water** | Impossible (swim = 1 tile, costs 30 Stamina) | Drowned regenerate. Ranged attacks fail. |
| **Ice** | Normal (risk of cracking) | Charge attacks deal +50% damage but risk breaking ice. |
| **Holy Ground** | Normal | Hostiles take 5 Holy damage per turn. Demons take 10. Player Faith regen +10%. |
| **Blight (high)** | Normal | Player gains 1 Corruption per turn. Hostiles regenerate 2 Health per turn. |
| **Structure (inside)** | Normal | Ranged attacks through walls impossible. Melee only at door. |

## 9. Death & The Chronicle

When Health reaches 0, you die. The Chronicle records the circumstances.

**Death types:**
| Type | Chronicle Entry | Legacy Effect |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| **Killed by beast** | "Torn apart by wolves in the Black Pines." | Next character finds bones. +1 arrow retrieved. |
| **Killed by demon** | "The Wendigo took him. We heard the screams." | Next character finds corrupted remains. Corruption +5 on pickup. |
| **Drowned** | "The lake does not give up its dead." | Next character finds body in water. Drowned spawn nearby. |
| **Corruption 100** | "He walked into the blight and did not stop." | Next character finds a Revenant (hostile, wearing your gear). |
| **Starvation** | "Winter took him slowly." | Next character finds frozen body. Food stores intact. |
| **Suicide (pilgrim)** | "She hung herself in the chapel." | Mood -20 for all pilgrims. Chapel temporarily desanctified. |

**Pilgrim death in combat:**
If a pilgrim dies, they drop what they were carrying. If not given Last Rites within 1 day, they rise as a Revenant and attack the settlement.

## 10. Weapon Degradation & The Craftsman

All weapons and armor have **durability** (not shown numerically to the player, but as condition states).

| Condition | Durability | Effect |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| **Pristine** | 100% | Full stats. |
| **Worn** | 75% | -10% damage / protection. |
| **Damaged** | 50% | -25% damage / protection. |
| **Broken** | 25% | -50% damage / protection. May jam/fail. |
| **Ruined** | 0% | Unusable. Must be repaired by Craftsman or discarded. |

**The Craftsman's repair:**
- Takes 1 turn and a hammer (tool).
- Restores 25% durability per repair.
- Costs 1 iron scrap (for metal) or 1 leather (for armor).
- Can repair mid-combat if he has action points.

**Silver weapons degrade twice as fast** (they are soft metal). A silver mace goes from Pristine to Ruined in about 20 swings. This makes the Craftsman essential for demon-hunting campaigns.

## 11. Technical Implications (Protocol)

The combat system requires these protocol additions:

| Command | Args | Description |
|---------|------|-------------|
| `COMBAT` | `start` / `end` | Combat mode entered/exited. Client may change UI. |
| `TURN` | `player` / `enemy` | Whose turn it is. |
| `ACT` | `type target` | Player used an action (attack, move, item, etc.). |
| `DAMAGE` | `x y amount type source` | Something took damage. |
| `STATUS` | `bleeding` / `panicked` / `frozen` etc. | Condition applied/removed. |
| `DEATH` | `entity type` | Something died. Triggers Chronicle entry. |
| `FEAR` | `source x y` | Fear effect triggered. Client may shake screen. |
| `CHANNEL` | `rite x y turns_remaining` | Priest is channeling. Vulnerable. |

## 12. Summary

| Vocation | Combat Role | Primary Weapon | Primary Defense | Key Tactic |
|----------|-------------|----------------|-----------------|------------|
| **Woodsman** | Striker / skirmisher | Bow, spear, axe | Speed, stealth | First strike, then vanish |
| **Priest** | Controller / support | Blessed crozier, holy water | Holy Ground, wards | Lure enemies into sanctified kill zones |
| **Craftsman** | Defender / engineer | War hammer, traps | Heavy armor, fortifications | Prepare the battlefield, endure the siege |

| System | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Six stats** | Simple, survival-focused. No RPG complexity. |
| **Four damage types** | Forces tactical weapon selection |
| **Turn-based, 2 AP** | Clean, readable, ASCII-friendly |
| **Degradation** | Craftsman essential. No infinite durability. |
| **Status effects** | Create desperation and hard choices |
| **Elevation/terrain** | Positioning matters as much as gear |
| **Manifestation fights** | Environmental puzzles, not DPS races |

The combat is not about being strong. It is about being **prepared, positioned, and desperate enough to survive.**
